About Us

Bekim Lumisolar--Inverter Repair and Solar Energy Specialist.

Technical Solar, Inverter & Energy Solutions in Kenya

Bekim Lumisolar Energy Limited is a Nairobi-based energy solutions company specializing in professional inverter repair, solar system design and installation, lithium battery services, and backup power solutions.

We provide technical diagnosis, repair, installation, commissioning and maintenance services for residential, commercial and institutional power systems.

Our focus is on proper system assessment, accurate diagnosis and appropriate equipment selection to provide practical and reliable energy solutions for our customers.

Our Technical Expertise

We specialize in the diagnosis, repair and servicing of solar and hybrid power equipment. Our technical expertise covers inverter faults, battery charging and discharging problems, PV and MPPT charging issues, control-board and protection-circuit faults, lithium battery BMS services, and solar system sizing and load assessment.

Our approach is based on proper testing and diagnosis to identify the source of a problem before recommending the appropriate repair or solution.

Our Solar & Energy Solutions

In addition to inverter repair, Bekim Lumisolar provides complete solar and backup power solutions for residential, commercial and institutional applications.

  • ☀️ Solar system design and sizing
  • ☀️ Hybrid and off-grid solar systems
  • ☀️ Solar panel supply and installation
  • 🔧 Inverter installation and servicing
  • 🔋 Lithium and lead-acid battery solutions
  • ⚡ Backup power and UPS solutions
  • 🛠️ Preventive maintenance
  • 📊 Energy audits and load assessment
