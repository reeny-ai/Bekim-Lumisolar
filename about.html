WHO WE ARE

Technical Expertise.
Reliable Energy Solutions.

Bekim Lumisolar Energy Limited is a Nairobi-based energy solutions company specializing in inverter repair, solar system design and installation, lithium battery services and backup power solutions.

We work with residential, commercial and institutional customers, providing technical assessment, diagnosis, repair, installation, commissioning and maintenance of solar and power systems.

Our approach is simple: understand the problem, diagnose it correctly and provide a practical solution that meets the customer's energy needs.

Our focus is on providing practical and reliable energy solutions based on proper system assessment, accurate diagnosis and appropriate equipment selection.

Complete Solar & Energy Solutions

Inverter Repair
