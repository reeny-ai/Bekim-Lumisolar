When Your Inverter Stops Working, We Find Out Why.

Bekim Lumisolar specializes in the diagnosis, repair and servicing of solar and hybrid inverters.

OUR TECHNICAL SERVICES

What We Repair & Troubleshoot

We diagnose a wide range of faults affecting solar, hybrid and backup power inverters.

Error Code Diagnosis

We investigate inverter error codes and identify the underlying cause of the fault.

No Output & Startup Faults

Troubleshooting inverters that fail to switch on or produce AC output.

Battery Charging Faults

Diagnosis of battery charging, discharging and related power-management problems.

PV & MPPT Problems

Troubleshooting solar input and MPPT charging problems affecting system performance.

Control Board Faults

Investigation of control-board, relay and protection-circuit faults.

Power Stage Faults

Troubleshooting DC bus, MOSFET, IGBT and other power-stage related faults.

TECHNICAL EXPERTISE

Repair Beyond the Error Code

An error code can indicate that something is wrong, but it does not always tell the complete story.

Our approach involves inspecting and testing the inverter and its relevant circuits to help identify the actual source of the problem.

  • Hybrid and off-grid inverter repair
  • DC bus and power-stage troubleshooting
  • MOSFET and IGBT-related faults
  • Control-board troubleshooting
  • Relay, fan and protection-circuit faults
  • Lithium battery BMS diagnosis
  • CAN and RS485 communication troubleshooting
