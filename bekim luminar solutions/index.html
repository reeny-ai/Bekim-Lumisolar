Company Overview

Bekim Lumisolar Energy Limited is a professional solar and inverter repair company in Nairobi, Kenya, specializing in inverter repair, troubleshooting, servicing, installation and maintenance.

We repair and service a wide range of solar inverters, hybrid inverters, off-grid inverters and UPS systems, helping homes, businesses, schools, churches and institutions restore reliable power and extend the life of their equipment.

Our services include inverter fault diagnosis and repair, battery testing and replacement, solar system troubleshooting, solar installation, inverter installation, preventive maintenance and backup power solutions.

Whether your inverter is beeping, showing an error code, failing to charge batteries, shutting down, producing no AC output, or experiencing other faults, our technical team can diagnose the problem and recommend the appropriate solution.

We also provide solar panels, lithium and lead-acid batteries, UPS systems, charge controllers and solar accessories.
